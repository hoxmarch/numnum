How to Play NumNum

Version 3.5

• Match Pairs: Select 2 numbers that are equal (e.g. 3 & 3) or add up to 10 (e.g. 7 & 3).

• Color Pairs: Matching pairs share harmonious chromatic family shades (e.g. 1 & 9, 2 & 8, 3 & 7, 4 & 6).

• Directions: Matches can be horizontal, vertical, diagonal, or wrapped from row to row.

• Undo Move: Made a mistake? Tap the Undo button to revert your last move!

• Add Numbers: Out of moves? Tap + Add to duplicate remaining numbers and keep playing.
