How to Play NumNum

Version 3.1

• Match Pairs: Select 2 numbers that are equal (e.g. 3 & 3) or add up to 10 (e.g. 7 & 3).

• Directions: Matches can be horizontal, vertical, diagonal, or wrapped from row to row.

• Stage Progression: Starts with 36 tiles in Stage 1 and adds 2 extra tiles every stage!

• Auto-Save: Game state, sound preferences, and remaining add charges auto-save continuously.
